## The Complete Workflow (End-to-End)

1. **Ingest** – Upload CSV/JSON feedback files or knowledge base documents (TXT/MD/PDF)
2. **Label** – AI automatically categorizes sentiment, category, and aspects
3. **Embed** – Transform text into semantic vectors for intelligent search
4. **Index** – Create unified search index across all your content
5. **Query** – Ask questions, search semantically, or explore analytics
6. **Act** – Use insights to improve products, prioritize features, enhance support
